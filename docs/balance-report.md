Generated by `pnpm sim --strategy active,idle --seeds 1,2,3 --hours 0.25,1,8`. Written by `tools/sim/run.ts`; do not edit by hand. "Total earned" milestones use lifetime `totalEarned`, not unspent points. "Income at end" is expected points per second: auto-roll plus the manual rolls the bot still makes. "not reached" means not within that horizon.
